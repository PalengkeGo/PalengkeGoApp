FILIPINO BREAKFAST
1. Tuyo
Region: Nationwide Category: Preserved Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
Sun-dried salted herring fried crispy — a humble Filipino pantry staple.
Calories: approximately 250 kcal per serving
Ingredients
3-4 pcs tuyo (dried salted herring)
1 tbsp cooking oil for frying
1 tbsp white vinegar
1 pc siling labuyo, sliced
1 clove garlic, minced (for dip)
Steps
Heat generous oil in pan over medium heat.
Fry tuyo 3-4 minutes per side until very crispy.
Drain on paper towels.
Make dipping vinegar: combine vinegar, garlic, and chili.
Serve tuyo with dipping vinegar and garlic rice.
Cooking Tips
Fry on medium heat — too high and exterior burns before the fish heats through.
Tuyo smell is strong; keep kitchen ventilated.
Gourmet tuyo in olive oil with chili is a modern palengke find — excellent on pandesal.
Pair with sliced tomatoes and bagoong to balance the saltiness.
2. Tapa
Region: Nationwide Category: Preserved Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving
Cured dried beef — marinated, air-dried, and pan-fried for breakfast bliss.
Ingredients
57g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
6 cloves garlic, minced
1/3 tsp soy sauce
2/3 tsp sugar
2/3 tsp calamansi juice
1/3 tsp salt
1/2/3 tsp black pepper
Steps
Slice beef against the grain as thin as possible.
Combine garlic, soy sauce, sugar, calamansi, salt, and pepper.
Toss beef in marinade. Refrigerate 24-48 hours.
Optional: air-dry in fridge uncovered for another 24 hours.
Pan-fry in minimal oil over medium-high heat until browned.
Serve as tapsilog: with sinangag (garlic rice) and itlog (fried egg).
Cooking Tips
Slicing beef paper-thin is key — freeze slightly before slicing.
The sugar helps caramelize and preserves the meat.
Longer marinating equals more flavorful tapa.
Tapsilog is one of the most loved Filipino breakfast combos.
3. Tapsilog
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving
Region: Nationwide Category: Breakfast Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
A classic Filipino breakfast consisting of marinated beef tapa, garlic fried rice (sinangag), and a sunny-side-up egg. It is one of the most popular "silog" meals enjoyed across the Philippines.
Ingredients
• 57g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
• 1/3 tsp soy sauce
• 2/3 tsp calamansi juice
• 1/3 tsp brown sugar
• 1 clove garlic, minced
• 1/6 tsp ground black pepper
• 2/3 tsp cooking oil
• 1/3 cup garlic fried rice
• 1 egg
Steps
Marinate the beef with soy sauce, calamansi juice, brown sugar, garlic, and black pepper for at least 4 hours or overnight.
Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
Fry the marinated beef until browned and cooked through.
In another pan, cook the sunny-side-up eggs.
Reheat or prepare the garlic fried rice.
Arrange the garlic rice, beef tapa, and egg on a serving plate.
Serve hot with sliced tomatoes or pickled papaya (atchara).
Cooking Tips
• Marinate the beef overnight for better flavor.
• Slice the beef thinly for tenderness.
• Cook over medium heat to prevent burning the marinade.
• Serve with vinegar or atchara for added flavor. Servings: 1 person
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving
4. Longsilog
Region: Luzon Category: Breakfast Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
A hearty Filipino breakfast made with sweet pork longganisa, garlic fried rice, and a fried egg. It is a favorite morning meal served in homes and eateries.
Ingredients
• 1 pieces pork longganisa
• 1/3 cup garlic fried rice
• 1 egg
• 1/3 tsp cooking oil
• Sliced tomatoes (optional)
Steps
Heat a pan over medium heat and add the longganisa.
Pour in ¼ cup water and cover the pan.
Cook until the water evaporates.
Add the cooking oil and fry the longganisa until browned.
Fry the eggs to your preferred doneness.
Prepare or reheat the garlic fried rice.
Serve together with sliced tomatoes.
Cooking Tips
• Cook the longganisa with water first to keep it juicy.
• Avoid high heat to prevent burning the sugar.
• Homemade garlic rice enhances the meal. Servings: 1 person
• Serve with vinegar for dipping.
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving

9. Champorado
Region: Nationwide Category: Breakfast Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
A comforting Filipino chocolate rice porridge traditionally served for breakfast, often paired with dried salted fish for a sweet and savory combination.
Ingredients
• 1/1 cup glutinous rice
• 1 2/1 cups water
• 1/6 cup cocoa powder or tablea

• 1/6 cup evaporated milk
Steps
Wash the glutinous rice thoroughly.
Bring the water to a boil in a pot.
Add the rice and cook until tender.
Stir in the cocoa powder or melted tablea.
Add the sugar and mix well.
Simmer until the porridge thickens. Servings: 1 person
Serve warm with evaporated milk drizzled on top.

Cooking Tips

• Use tablea for a richer chocolate flavor.
• Adjust the sugar according to your preference.
• Serve with tuyo for a traditional pairing.
10. Arroz Caldo
Region: Luzon Category: Breakfast Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
A warm and comforting Filipino rice porridge cooked with chicken, ginger, and garlic. It is a popular breakfast and rainy-day meal.
Ingredients
• 1/1 cup glutinous rice
• 57g chicken, cut into serving pieces
• 2/3 cups chicken broth
• 1/3 onion, chopped
• 1 clove garlic, minced
• 1/3 thumb-sized ginger, julienned
• 2/3 tsp fish sauce
• 2/3 tsp cooking oil
• Chopped spring onions and boiled eggs for garnish
Steps
Heat oil and sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger until fragrant.
Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
Stir in the fish sauce.
Add the glutinous rice and chicken broth.
Bring to a boil, then simmer until the rice is soft and the porridge thickens. Servings: 1 person
Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving
Garnish with spring onions and boiled eggs before serving.
Cooking Tips
• Stir occasionally to prevent the rice from sticking.
• Fresh ginger gives the broth a richer flavor.
• Add fried garlic for extra aroma.
• Serve hot with calamansi and fish sauce.

18. Lugaw
Region: Nationwide Category: Breakfast Difficulty: Easy Servings: 1 person
A simple and comforting Filipino rice porridge cooked with garlic, ginger, and onions. It is commonly served as a light breakfast with boiled eggs, fried garlic, and spring onions.
Ingredients
• 1 cup white rice
• 7 cups chicken broth or water
• 1 onion, chopped
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 1 thumb-sized ginger, sliced
• 2 tbsp fish sauce Servings: 1 person
• 2 tbsp cooking oil
Calories: approximately 600 kcal per serving
• Fried garlic, chopped spring onions, and boiled eggs for garnish
Steps
Heat oil and sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger until fragrant.
Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes.
Pour in the chicken broth or water and bring to a boil.
Reduce the heat and simmer for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally until the rice breaks down into a creamy porridge.
Stir in the fish sauce and adjust the seasoning.
Ladle the lugaw into serving bowls.
Garnish with fried garlic, spring onions, and boiled eggs before serving.
Cooking Tips
• Stir frequently to prevent the rice from sticking to the pot.
• Add more broth if the porridge becomes too thick.
• Top with fried garlic for extra flavor and crunch.
• Serve hot with calamansi and fish sauce on the side.
