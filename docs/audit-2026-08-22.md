## 1. Executive verdict

**Overall grade: B− (7.0 / 10) — excellent engineering discipline, not shippable as-is.**

| Aspect | One-liner |
|---|---|
| Strength | Trusted-backend design (server-side pricing/stock/fees, HMAC webhooks, transactional stock, audit trails) is genuinely above-average — better than most production Flutter apps. |
| Weakness | The trusted backend is **partially unreachable**: the client never initiates online payments, review reads come from mock data, and two money-integrity holes remain open in Firestore rules. |
| Risk if shipped today | Orders with attacker-controlled prices (H1), fake "GCash-paid" orders that never settle (H2), illegal terminal states under concurrency (H3), cancelled orders that never restock (M4). |

**The pattern across every finding:** the *design* is right, the *wiring* is incomplete. This is a
codebase mid-migration from mock-demo to production — the skeleton is production-grade, the flesh
still has demo seams.
