# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PalengkeGo serves two primary user groups equally:

- customers who browse local market stalls, compare fresh goods, build carts, place orders, and track fulfillment
- vendors who manage stall visibility, product listings, stock state, and order preparation inside the same ecosystem

Customers use the product as a faster, clearer way to shop from a local public market without losing the feeling of browsing real stalls. Vendors use it as a lightweight operating surface for daily market work, not as a heavy enterprise dashboard.

Both groups are often using the app in practical, time-sensitive contexts:

- customers on mobile, checking stalls, products, prices, and order status quickly
- vendors between real-world transactions, needing clear order and inventory actions with minimal friction

## Product Purpose

PalengkeGo translates the experience of a Filipino local market into a dependable digital product. It should preserve the personality, warmth, and community feeling of a real palengke while making ordering, browsing, and vendor operations easier to understand and faster to complete.

Success means:

- customers trust the app enough to use it as a practical shopping tool, not just a concept demo
- vendors can manage orders and products with simple, low-stress flows
- the product feels rooted in the market experience rather than like a generic ecommerce clone
- the interface is clear enough for repeat daily use, especially on mobile-sized screens

## Brand Personality

The product should feel:

- grounded
- dependable
- clear

This is a clean utilitarian brand direction, not a loud campaign surface. The product should feel calm, legible, and competent, with just enough market character to avoid feeling sterile. It should communicate trust and usefulness first, then warmth and local identity through tone, color, and imagery.

## Anti-references

PalengkeGo should not look like:

- a generic startup dashboard with cold blue-gray SaaS styling
- a luxury lifestyle marketplace that feels too polished, premium, or detached from everyday market use
- an over-decorated “AI landing page” style product full of ornamental gradients, excessive cards, or dramatic motion
- a dark-mode-first developer tool UI

It should also avoid flows that feel confusingly “creative” when users are trying to complete routine tasks like checking stock, adding to cart, or reviewing an order.

## Design Principles

1. Familiar market actions should feel easier, not abstracted away.
2. Utility comes first, but utility should still feel warm and human.
3. Trust is earned through consistency, clarity, and truthful data.
4. Mobile-first structure is non-negotiable because the product lives in quick, practical use.
5. Customer and vendor surfaces should feel like parts of one product, not separate unrelated apps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

PalengkeGo should aim for practical WCAG AA accessibility in its core flows.

Important considerations:

- maintain strong text contrast in all primary shopping and management views
- keep touch targets comfortable on mobile
- avoid relying on color alone for status meaning
- keep motion purposeful and limited
- use clear labels and straightforward language for both customers and vendors
- support users who may be less familiar with dense digital commerce interfaces
